I am a researcher and entrepreneur specializing in **machine learning, genomics, and drug discovery**. My work focuses on developing **large-scale self-supervised models** to enable biological insights and therapeutic discoveries. I am passionate about building **foundation models** for single-cell omics and molecular biology, integrating generative AI with experimental pipelines to accelerate biomedical breakthroughs.

I completed my **Ph.D. in Computer Science** at the **University of Toronto** (2019-2024) under **Prof. Bo Wang**, where I pioneered **scGPT**, one of the first generative foundation models for single-cell multi-omics. I also led the development of **COMPILE**, an **AI-driven self-driving lab** for mRNA therapeutics.
